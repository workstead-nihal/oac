# Community Code of Conduct — Odisha Anime Community (OAC)

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, inclusive, and welcoming atmosphere, we as members, organizers, and contributors of the **Odisha Anime Community (OAC)** pledge to make participation in our community, events, meetups, online chats, and digital repositories a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of anime fandom, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Respect & Kindness**: Treating all fans, cosplayers, artists, gamers, and newcomers with empathy and respect.
- **Cosplay is Not Consent**: Always asking permission before taking photos of cosplayers or touching outfits/props.
- **Welcoming New Fans**: Embracing otaku at all stages of their journey — from those who just watched their first anime to lifelong fans.
- **Constructive Engagement**: Providing helpful, respectful feedback on community art, cosplay, and creative work.
- **Community Safety**: Keeping event spaces, discord channels, and community groups safe, respectful, and family-friendly.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, doxxing, or sharing non-consensual photography.
- Gatekeeping or shaming others for their anime preferences, skill level, or cosplay budget.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or friendly community setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The community leadership team and event moderators are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (online servers, WhatsApp groups, social media channels, conventions, meetups) as well as when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

---

## Reporting Guidelines

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the community leadership team at:

- **Email**: `info@joinoac.in`
- **Instagram**: `@odisha.anime.community`
- **Discord**: Contact an active Server Administrator or Moderator.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Confidentiality of the reporter will be strictly maintained.
